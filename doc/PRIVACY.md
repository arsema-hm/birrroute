# BirrRoute privacy policy (template: review before publishing)
BirrRoute does not require an account and does not collect personal information.
Amounts, saved transfers, saved complaint cases and your language choice are stored only on your phone and never sent to us.
If you tap "Report a wrong fee", your email app opens with the route details; nothing is sent unless you send it.
If remote data updates are enabled, the app downloads a public fee file; no information about you is uploaded.
BirrRoute gives information only, not financial advice. Fees change: confirm with your bank before sending.
