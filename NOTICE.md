## Data sources

- Live bank dollar rates: ET-Forex (https://et-forex.com), used through its public API with credit, under its terms.
- Official NBE exchange rate: AllRatesToday (https://allratestoday.com), used with credit.
- Bank fees: each bank's own published tariff pages, with the date checked shown in the app.
